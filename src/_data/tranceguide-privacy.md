### Privacy Policy for TranceGuide

Effective date: August 18, 2025

TranceGuide helps you discover trance and electronic music events. We take your privacy seriously and only collect the minimum data necessary to provide core functionality.

#### Data We Collect and Why
- Location (approximate or precise, depending on your device settings):

  - Purpose: Show nearby events and center the map around you when you ask (Around Me filter or tapping the location button).
  - How: Your coordinates may be sent as query parameters to the GoaBase.net API to return relevant events.
  - Storage: Not stored on TranceGuide servers. Coordinates are not kept on device beyond in-memory use for the active session and request.
  - Linked to you: No.

- Local app preferences and caches (on your device only):

  - UserDefaults: theme selection, and whether you prefer location-based filtering.
  - API response cache: event lists and event details cached on-device for short periods to improve performance.
  - Image cache: publicly available event flyer images cached on-device; filenames are hashed and not tied to your identity.

We do not collect your name, email, phone number, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, health, fitness, or financial information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs, and we do not track you across apps or websites.

#### Third‑Party Services
- GoaBase.net API (read-only):

  - Used to fetch event data. When you request nearby results, your location (coordinates and radius) is sent to this API to return relevant events.
  - GoaBase may receive standard network metadata (e.g., IP address) when responding to your request. Refer to GoaBase’s own policies for their processing practices.

#### Data Retention
- API cache: event list/detail responses cached on-device with a short lifetime (about 10 minutes) and refreshed as needed.
- Image cache: stored on-device and periodically cleaned up based on size limits.
- Preferences: stored until you change them or delete the app.
- We do not operate a backend for TranceGuide and therefore do not retain your personal data on our servers.

#### Security
- All network requests use HTTPS.
- Cached data is stored locally on your device. No sensitive personal information is cached.

#### Your Choices
- Location: You can grant or revoke “While Using the App” location permission at any time in iOS Settings. If you deny permission, nearby event features are disabled.
- Clear local data: Deleting the app removes all locally stored caches and preferences.

#### Changes to This Policy
We may update this policy from time to time. We will update the “Effective date” above for any changes.
